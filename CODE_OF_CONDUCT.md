# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of identity, background, experience or research institution. Offer specific, constructive criticism and respect scholarly disagreement. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sharing private or sensitive heritage information are unacceptable.

Respect cultural communities, provenance, ownership and access restrictions. Do not use this community to facilitate looting, illicit trade or disclosure of protected excavation locations.

Maintainers may edit/remove content, restrict participation or ban repeated or serious violations. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel; if none is available, request one without posting private details. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, disclose conflicts of interest and apply proportionate action. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and official project spaces.
